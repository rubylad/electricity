Key Insights & Findings

nights_stay Evening Peak Consumption

Peak consumption occurs during evening hours (18:30-22:00), with the highest average of 0.65 units at 18:30-19:00. This represents a 220% increase from the daily low point, indicating significant evening activity.

ac_unit Winter Consumption Spike

The highest consumption days cluster in winter months (November-February), with 28 Nov 2024 reaching 26.68 units. This suggests heating-related electricity usage during colder periods.

bedtime Low Overnight Usage

Consumption drops to minimal levels (below 0.20 units) during overnight hours (00:30-06:00), representing baseline appliance usage and standby power consumption.

wb_sunny Consistent Daily Pattern

A predictable daily pattern emerges: gradual morning increase (06:00-10:30), stable daytime plateau (10:30-18:00), sharp evening peak (18:30-22:00), then decline overnight. This consistency enables accurate forecasting.
